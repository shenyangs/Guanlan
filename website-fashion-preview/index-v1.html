Install Explore signal

Search gives an agent links. Guanlan gives it provenance, platform context, source roles, freshness boundaries, and evidence packets it can reason with.

01

Designed for evidence, not traffic.

Route

Find the right waterway.

Policy, commerce, developer forums, entertainment, hotboards, and original pages are routed differently.

Read

Bring back clean material.

Public pages are read into compact, source-aware context with quality notes and fallback traces.

Research

Turn fragments into packets.

Evidence roles, source mix, recency signals, and next-step advice arrive as agent-ready structure.

02

One command, many surfaces.

guanlan research "国产 AI 应用 出海" --profile china --limit 80 --advisor
China-aware Profiles and scopes respect local platform structure.
Agent-ready Markdown, JSON, MCP, and local workflow outputs.
Read-only first Public search and reading before any authenticated action.
Traceable Quality signals explain when evidence is thin.
